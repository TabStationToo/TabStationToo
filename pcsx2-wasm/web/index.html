About TabStationToo.

  
TabStationToo runs PCSX2, the PlayStation 2 emulator, compiled to WebAssembly, entirely in this tab.

  
Use only a BIOS dumped from a console you own, and disc images of games you own. The BIOS is kept in this browser (IndexedDB), never sent anywhere, and you can forget it from the BIOS menu. A disc image is read from your file as the game needs it; nothing is copied or uploaded.

  
Works today: the BIOS menus and games from disc images, with sound, a keyboard and gamepads, and memory cards kept in this browser or in files on your disk. Many games run at or near full speed, the heaviest scenes still below. Save states are kept in this browser.
